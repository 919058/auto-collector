## Why Android Apps on ChromeOS?

ChromeOS can run Android apps if your device supports it. This gives you access to powerful auto clicker apps that:
- ✅ Work system-wide (not just in browser)
- ✅ No installation required (download from Play Store)
- ✅ Easy to use interface
- ✅ Customizable hotkeys
- ✅ No Linux knowledge needed
- ✅ Works on school computers (usually allowed)

---

## Step 1: Check if Your ChromeOS Supports Android Apps

1. Click the **time** in bottom-right corner
2. Click **Settings** ⚙️
3. Go to **Advanced** → **Developers**
4. Look for **Linux development environment** or **Google Play Store**

**If you see "Google Play":**
- Click **Turn On** (or it might already be enabled)
- Your ChromeOS supports Android apps

**If you don't see it:**
- Your device may not support Android apps
- You can skip to the **Web Version** section below

---

## Step 2: Enable Google Play Store

1. Go to **Settings** → **Advanced** → **Developers**
2. Click **Turn on** next to "Google Play Store"

## How to Use - General Steps

Most Android auto clicker apps work similarly:

### Basic Setup:
1. **Open the app**
2. **Set Click Interval** (milliseconds between clicks)
   - Example: 100ms = fast clicking
   - Example: 500ms = slower clicking
3. **Set Total Clicks**
   - 0 or blank = infinite clicking
   - Number = stop after that many clicks
4. **Set Hotkey**
   - Tap "Set Key" or "Toggle Key"
   - Press your chosen key
   - Examples: F6, Q, A, Space, Volume buttons

### Start Clicking:
1. Tap the **Start** or **Begin** button
2. Move to where you want to click
3. Press your hotkey to start
4. Press your hotkey again to stop

## Detailed Setup Guide - OP Auto Clicker (Most Popular)

### Installation:
1. Search "OP Auto Clicker" in Play Store
2. Install it

### First Time Setup:

**Step 1: Set Click Interval**
- Tap **Interval** or **Delay**
- Enter milliseconds (100 = 0.1 seconds)
- Recommended: 50-200ms for normal use

**Step 2: Set Hotkey**
- Tap **Set Hotkey** or **Key Settings**
- Press the key you want (F6, Volume Up, etc.)
- Tap **OK**

**Step 3: Set Click Location**
- Tap **Set Position** or **Choose Location**
- Tap on the screen where you want to click
- Or tap **Follow Cursor** to click where your finger is

**Step 4: Set Total Clicks**
- Tap **Clicks** or **Tap Times**
- Enter number (0 = infinite)

### Start Clicking:
1. Tap **Start** button
2. Press your hotkey
3. Watch it click!
4. Press hotkey again to stop

## Troubleshooting

### "App won't click"
- Make sure app has **Accessibility permissions**
  - Settings → Apps → [App Name] → Permissions → Accessibility
  - Turn ON

### "Hotkey not working"
- App might need to be running in background
- Make sure Accessibility is enabled (see above)
- Try a different key

### "App crashes on school computer"
- School might have restrictions
- Try "Automatic Clicker - AutoTap" (lighter app)
- Or use the web version instead

### "Can't find app in Play Store"
- School computer might have restrictions
- Check if Google Play Store is available
- Try downloading .apk file directly (advanced)
